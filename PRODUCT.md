# Gutter Guys — product

- **Platform:** web
- **Stack:** static single-file HTML/CSS/JS (no framework, no tracking), hosted on GitHub Pages
- **Surface mode:** Persuade (one-page business site)

## Users
- **First:** the owner of Gutter Guys, who will see this link on a phone inside Facebook's in-app browser. The page has to read as their own business, done properly, within one screen.
- **Second:** homeowners (and some commercial property owners — flyer says "Residential / Commercial") who need gutters, eavestroughs, roofs or downspouts cleaned, usually in fall leaf-drop season. They arrive on a phone, one-handed.

## Purpose
Turn a curious visitor into a call or a text for a free quote.

## Positioning
A gutter, eavestrough and roof cleaning crew that clears the job and leaves the property clean. Their own lines: "Clean gutters, protect your home!", "We gutter done!", "Reliable service. Quality work. Clean results."

## Capabilities (from the brief and their flyer)
- Gutter & eavestrough cleaning; eavestrough / downspout flushing
- Roof cleaning & moss removal
- Soffit & fascia cleaning; full exterior cleaning
- Minor gutter repairs
- Complete ground cleanup
- Free quotes by phone

## Constraints
- Service area unknown: 519 area code (southwestern Ontario) but advertised in a New Jersey group. **Publish no location.**
- Whether the numbers accept texts is unknown; calling must always be available.
- No email, hours, owner name, prices or reviews known: say nothing about them.
- The flyer's before/after tiles look AI-generated and are ~90px each: not used as proof of real jobs.

## Brand commitments
- Logo: "GUTTER GUYS / GUTTER CLEANING" badge — royal blue, black and silver, house roofline, worker clearing leaves, three water drops.
- Voice: "we" (a crew). Urgent seasonal, plain.
- Ground: deep navy (never black), royal blue from the logo.
